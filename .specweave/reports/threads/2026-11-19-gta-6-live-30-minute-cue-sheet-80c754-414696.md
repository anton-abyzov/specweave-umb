# GTA 6 live: 30-minute cue sheet

November 19, 2026. Title: **I Let AI Make Every Choice in My First Hour of GTA 6 🔴 LIVE**. Thumbnail: [kie-B-1280x720.jpg](/Users/anton/Projects/github/specweave-umb/.specweave/reports/threads/2026-11-19-gta6-live/kie-B-1280x720.jpg).

## Say this first

> "This is my first hour in GTA 6. It came out today. For the next thirty minutes I don't make the decisions. The AI does. Every time the game gives me a choice, the AI picks, I do it on the controller, and you get ten seconds in chat to overrule it."

## The rule for every decision

1. Anton reads out the choice on screen, or describes the fork.
2. The AI answers with **one letter** from a fixed list (A, B or C). No essays.
3. Chat has 10 seconds. A clear majority for something else wins once per segment.
4. Anton executes on the **DualSense**. The AI never touches the game.

## Prompt to paste into the AI (literal)

```text
You are deciding for me in GTA 6. Reply with exactly one letter and at most 8 words why.
Situation: <what is on screen>
A) <option 1>
B) <option 2>
C) <option 3, or "play it safe">
If you are unsure, answer C.
```

## Timeline

| Time | Segment | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 2:00 | Opening | Say the opening line. Show the controller in hand. Pin the chat rule. |
| 2:00 to 8:00 | First choices | Walk, talk, follow prompts. First A/B/C call at the first fork or dialogue choice. |
| 8:00 to 15:00 | Quick decisions | One call every two to three minutes: which way, which car, help or ignore. Read each AI letter out loud before doing it. |
| 15:00 to 16:00 | Check-in | "Guides say GTA 6 rates what kind of criminal you are; let's see where the AI lands." Check the in-game profile icon before naming anything. |
| 16:00 to 25:00 | Chat vs AI | Same loop, one chat overrule allowed. Count AI wins vs chat wins on screen or out loud. |
| 25:00 to 28:00 | Last call | One bigger choice. AI picks; chat gets its final overrule. |
| 28:00 to 30:00 | Close | Score: AI calls vs chat overrules. "Next stream: should the AI drive the controller too?" |

## When the AI is slow or silent

- **No answer in 10 seconds:** chat decides. Say "AI timed out, chat, you're up."
- **Answer outside A, B or C, or a refusal:** take C (play it safe) and keep moving.
- **Chat is quiet too:** Anton picks and says so on air.

## Available today vs optional

| Piece | Status |
|---|---|
| AI picks a letter in chat, Anton plays on DualSense | **Available today.** Any chat model works with the prompt above. |
| AI presses buttons in the game | **Not tonight.** PS Remote Play ignores the Mac keyboard; only the PS button click was shown to work once. |

| OpenAI Decisions for the pick | **Optional, off.** Not set up in this project and no `OPENAI_API_KEY` in this shell. |

What OpenAI's own docs say about Decisions ([Decisions guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/decisions), read November 19, 2026): it is generally available at `POST /v1/decisions`, `gpt-6-luna` is the only supported model, and a `choice` question returns one of your supplied values with probabilities. Input can include images, so a screenshot plus "A, B or C" fits it. The guide also lists a native GPT-Live integration. None of this is wired up for tonight; it is a next-stream idea and needs calibration first.
