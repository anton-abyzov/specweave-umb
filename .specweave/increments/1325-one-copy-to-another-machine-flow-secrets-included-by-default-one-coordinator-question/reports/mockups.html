Copy a project to another machine: three options

Today five menu items (Add to another machine, Move to another machine, Move to m5, Sync to m5, the Add or move button) open one dialog with a Hand off / Copy local files toggle, a Workspace locations table and secrets left out. Under the hood every path is one copy engine plus one switch: does the coordinator stay or move. Each option below makes that switch the only question.

A. One dialog, one coordinator question Recommended

One menu item, "Copy to another machine…". Files, secrets and uncommitted work come along by default; one question says where the coordinator runs.
