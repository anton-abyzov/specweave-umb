Leave this machine: Away mode

You take one action (menu, ⌥⌘L, /away or $away) and get one plan. Running and queued threads, routines and the coordinator move to Ministorm. You get one summary, and Bring back undoes it when the laptop returns. Missing grants are found before anything moves.

The flow

1
PlanTarget is preselected (remembered). Every thread, routine and coordinator gets a row: moves, stays (with reason) or paused.
2 · gate
Access checkMissing grants, including routines on Ministorm, project links, sign-ins and .env files. One Request access button covers all of them.
3
CoordinatorPlanned handover: Ministorm takes over, the M4 gives way and workers are adopted. Never two active copies.
4
RoutinesRun state and output files go along. The M4 copy is disabled before the Ministorm copy is enabled.
5
ThreadsRunning and queued threads move, queue included. One that can't move stays handoff-ready with a reason.
6
SummaryMoved, stayed and why. Saved on the server, so it shows on wake and on your phone.
↩
Bring backReverses all of it and re-arms Ministorm as standby. Grants are never revoked.

Lid close: the M4 has 5 seconds before sleep, so it only records an "away intent" on Ministorm. Ministorm finishes the move from its side after the grace period, even with no window open.

Three choices for you

1. Closing the lid

When should sleep start Away mode by itself?

A
On battery only, after 2 minutesRecommendedClosing the lid at your desk on power changes nothing. Away from the desk, work moves if the lid stays shut for 2 minutes.
B
Every sleep, after 2 minutesThe most coverage. A lid closed at your desk also moves work.
C
Never automaticallyOnly the menu, hotkey or /away start it.

2. Bringing work back

What happens when the M4 wakes up after Away?

A
Ask on wake, one clickRecommendedA card shows the summary and a "Bring back" button. Work you leave on Ministorm keeps running there.
B
Automatically on powerOnce the M4 has been awake on power for 5 minutes, everything returns. Every return adds another move to each thread.
C
Only when I askMenu or /back. No prompt.

3. Which coordinators move

Each project's coordinator move needs a project link and a grant on Ministorm.

A
Only projects with live workRecommendedProjects with running or queued threads or enabled routines. Idle projects stay on the M4 and need no new grants.
B
Every project on this machineFully hands off, but the first run asks for links and grants for every project.

Already decided (reversible)

  • One remembered target, shared with the existing "When this machine sleeps" setting. The first ready always-on machine is suggested.
  • /away, $away and /back are built into the app, so they work in every chat and on every machine without installing a skill.
  • Webhook routines stay paused here: their URL belongs to the machine.
  • A thread that can't move is never stopped, only left handoff-ready with a reason.
