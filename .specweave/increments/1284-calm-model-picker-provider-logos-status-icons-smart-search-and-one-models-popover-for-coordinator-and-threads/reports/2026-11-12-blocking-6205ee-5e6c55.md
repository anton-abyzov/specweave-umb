## Blocking

**1. `NAME_INSIDE` lists unrelated models for common queries** — `apps/web/src/components/chat/modelPickerSearch.ts:78` (`if (token.length >= 4 && field.includes(token)) return NAME_INSIDE;`)

My probe at the head (`/private/tmp/review-1284-r2/src/apps/web/src/components/chat/lead.probe.test.ts`):

```
"mini" -> GPT-5 mini | o4-mini | Gemini 3 Flash | Gemini 3 Pro | Gemini 3 Pro | Gemini 4 Carbon   (Gemini rows: highlight [])
"nnet" -> Claude Sonnet 5.5   (highlight [])
"seek" -> DeepSeek V3         (highlight [])
```

- "mini" sits inside "ge**mini**", so every Gemini row matches with nothing highlighted. Against a realistic catalog the reviewer counted 14 such rows.
- "google mini" and "cursor mini" return only Gemini rows.
- "destral" lists Codestral as a direct match, so the typo fallback at `:240` never runs and Devstral stays hidden (D-06).
- This fails AC-05 ("only matching models are listed, the matched characters are highlighted") and the check you named. Round-1 head had no such tier; main's old fuzzy match behaved the same way.
- Suggested fix: drop `includes` and split camel-case humps on the original-case name ("DeepSeek" becomes "deep seek"), plus a `seek` alias for lowercase slugs. Add a test that "mini" lists no Gemini row.
