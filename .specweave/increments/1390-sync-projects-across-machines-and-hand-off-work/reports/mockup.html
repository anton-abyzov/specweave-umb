Machines sync files. Work is handed off.

Proposal for increment 1390, revised after the first review. The words Main and Copy leave the interface. A project lives on several machines as equals, and three verbs cover everything you do between them. Mockup with specweave's machines and the M4's Git state of November 18. Nothing here is built.

filesSyncGit first: each machine fetches and fast-forwards, and a missing repository is cloned. Nothing is pushed. Work Git does not carry is offered as extras.
files, first timeAddPut the project on a machine that does not have it: download from GitHub, plus optional extras from the source machine.
workHand offThreads, unsaved work, the coordinator conversation and its routines continue on another machine. By hand, or by itself on sleep or at a limit.

What a hand off does, in order

1 CheckNothing moves. Sizes are measured, and every check the coordinator's move needs runs now.
2 SyncM5 fetches and fast-forwards.
3 CoordinatorThe conversation moves. If M5 cannot take it, nothing has moved yet and you choose.
4 RoutinesWith their run state, so none repeats or skips a run.
5 ThreadsOne at a time, each as a hidden checkpoint. A thread across several repositories goes whole or stays.
6 Folder workThe snapshot branch. m4 keeps every file; "Bring work back" reverses it.
