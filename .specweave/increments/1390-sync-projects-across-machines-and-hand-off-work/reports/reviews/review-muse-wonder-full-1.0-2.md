R2-01 in full: the design's central sequence has no executor. Today a person pressing "Make X main" runs a window-driven dance: the window calls `machineHandoff.prepare` on both servers, holds the preview digest, presents consent, publishes, calls receive, then ack (`ProjectMachineHandoff.tsx:196-197` and the `MachineHandoff.ts` guards at `:846-877`). Away's runner, by contrast, is server-side precisely so a lid close needs no window (`AwayRunner.ts:1-33`). AC-21 merges the two without saying which lifetime governs: if the window drives, closing the laptop after pressing Hand off (the headline use) suspends the driver mid-protocol, possibly after the fence at `MachineHandoff.ts:1008-1021`; if the server drives, a peer transport, its authentication (work grant, project in grant), its digest consent and its replay rules are all new design surface in Phase 3, none of it in AC-33, the Approach or T-12's files. The spec must pick one and design it; "the existing git-sync move" (`spec.md:175`) is not an existing server step.

R2-03 in full: the idle check the design relaxes was load-bearing for more than thread counting. With every thread idle, source HEADs, the coordinator context and the destination ancestry all stand still between preview and publish, which is what `MachineHandoff.ts:968-975` and `handoffGit.ts:1144-1148` assert. Coordinator-first with ticked threads still running removes that stillness from both ends at once: source threads commit (their job, during a Sync that fast-forwards 99 commits and clones), and step 2 itself moves the target. Either step 3 re-prepares (and the check step's per-row results and the push consent the person ticked describe a different move), or it does not (and the common case refuses `source_changed` after a clean check). A ticked thread also becomes waiting in this window by asking a question; the leave-out list then waives exactly the thread `folderCopyPreparation.ts:67-72` says blocks because "moving would drop it", and step 5 must either drop the question or strand the thread post-move. The design needs a stated freeze or re-check rule, not only a leave-out list.
