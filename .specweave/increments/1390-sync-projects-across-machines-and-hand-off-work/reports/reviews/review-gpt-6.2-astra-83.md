## Blocking finding: grant expiry at read admission

Expiry is checked before the asynchronous authority decision. The new post-await check re-reads the grant and checks revocation, but not expiry against the current time. A new read can share a decision started before the deadline, so this is not merely an already-admitted response finishing late. A still-authenticated peer can receive its copy's manifest after its grant expires. See [shared authority decision:195–198,432–435,496–501](/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanSource.ts#L496) and [manifest admission:536–556](/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanListener.ts#L536). Recheck current grant validity with the current clock before admitting each read, including reads sharing a pending decision. This blocks under the requested authorization-bypass rule; it does not mean a replaced bearer works again.
