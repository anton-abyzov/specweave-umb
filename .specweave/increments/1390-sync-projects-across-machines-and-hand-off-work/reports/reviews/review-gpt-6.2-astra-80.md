**1. Grant rotation.** The LAN read captures the grant, awaits live policy, then, if its use timestamp is over a minute old, saves the *whole captured grant*. A matching nonterminal re-offer can replace the bearer during that await. The save serializes disk writes, not the read/modify/write decision. The old hash can become current again and the newly issued bearer can stop working. The public re-offer caller treats a busy check as a warning, not a prohibition ([caller:607–644](/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/MachineHandoff.ts); [bearer verification:95–112](/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/transferGrant.ts)). Update only the timestamp of the still-current, still-authorized grant; make rotation and the conditional update atomic.

**2. One-way switch.** The durable record conflates the highest *checked* generation with the last *accepted* generation. A higher check clears `accepted` even if it finds no usable address. A Connect data read then has no accepted predecessor to latch. If a replacement offer subsequently succeeds, LAN resumes after that read. S-12 permits this only if the replacement was **accepted before** the Connect data read. Preserve those two facts separately and commit the transport decision under the operation lock. Also cancel pending dials on teardown: `destroy()` visits the pool, but `open()` can finish an outstanding await, insert a socket and send `hello` after destruction ([connector:107–115,181–218,370–373](/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanConnector.ts)).
