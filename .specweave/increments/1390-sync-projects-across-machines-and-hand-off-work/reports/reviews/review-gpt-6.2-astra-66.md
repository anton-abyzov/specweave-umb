## Blocking findings

1. **High: established LAN reads can outlive approval.** Connect checks live team rules, device approval and grant expiry. LAN calls the readers after checking recorded revocation, terminal state and root identity. Removing approval or reaching expiry need not stop an established read. The fixture injects `deny()` instead of withdrawing real authority. Use a shared live authorization decision; persist a denial before closing both paths and cut in-flight responses. `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/MachineHandoff.ts:803–826`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/transferGrant.ts:95–112`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/sourceService.ts:338–385`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanSource.ts:370–380`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/twoServer.lan.test.ts:499–522`.

2. **High: losing generations can publish keys or connectors.** The generation comparison precedes the locked update; a no-op save need not prevent publication. After restart a higher check can arrive before recovery of accepted-but-keyless state. The accepted candidate set is not retained for the final offer. Make check, offer and recovery atomic; publish only for the winning generation and its approved hosts. `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanDestination.ts:196–245,261–311,336–354`. The negative test retains the prior generation binding even for the nominal control case; changing its address to `203.0.113.9` does not isolate address validation. Start from a valid same-generation positive control. `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/twoServer.lan.test.ts:418–446`.

3. **High, AC-03/17: recipient rejection happens after handshake completion.** The listener requests a certificate but compares the pin in `secureConnection`. Closing there prevents HTTP but is not a failed TLS handshake. Require recipient identity during TLS verification and distinguish handshake rejection from zero HTTP bytes. TLS 1.3 and ALPN are enforced; rejecting reused sessions before HTTP is not proof that tickets, resumption and early data are disabled. `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanListener.ts:192–206,331–350`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanListener.test.ts:260–297,317–345`.

4. **High: Connect is not behind one durable transition.** Expiry or opt-out can return Connect without latching; restart can reach Connect validation before lost-key recovery. After wrong pin or identity the comparison uses file metadata and whole-file hashes, not every retained chunk hash from the fresh Connect manifest. Make workers, pending dials and recovery observe one durable transition; check staged chunk hashes before reuse. `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanDestination.ts:169–177,336–390,432–461`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/sourceClient.ts:423–467`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/twoServer.lan.test.ts:539–555`.

5. **High: the approved source scope is not fixed for the operation.** The binding uses all non-secret classes rather than the approved ceiling. Root identity is sampled when each listener is published, so a replacement between approval and publication becomes the new baseline. A refreshed chunk index can diverge from the published manifest. Persist the approved ceiling and root identity at approval; serve the index belonging to that generation. `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanWire.ts:200–212`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanSource.ts:341–379`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/sourceService.ts:1139–1181,1279–1305`.

6. **High: negotiation and retirement are not bilateral.** Candidate addresses can be sent before support and opt-in are known at the other end. Destination opt-out or fallback closes local connections but does not notify the source to retire its listener within one second. A route-only change need not change the interface fingerprint. Negotiate capability and consent before disclosing candidates; propagate authenticated retirement and check both ends on opt-out, route change and withdrawal. `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanSource.ts:79–88,255–279`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanDestination.ts:133–177,368–381`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/lanInterfaces.ts:175–185`; `/Users/antonabyzov/Projects/github/specweave-umb/repositories/anton-abyzov/specwithcode/apps/server/src/fleet/folderTransfer/twoServer.lan.test.ts:669–711`.

7. **High, AC-28/29: limits and deadlines are not all established at the socket boundary.** The `202` response cap is applied after buffering; the write-stall check measures locally queued bytes rather than demonstrated forward progress.
