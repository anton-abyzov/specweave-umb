If the secondary frame succeeds and the connection drops before the primary frame, the secondary repository retains a reachable commit containing its unpushed and uncommitted work. There is no set-level abort or expiry here. No thread being launched is not the same as no member having moved. Stage under a transfer identity and clean up aborted or expired transfers; check the destination refs after cutting the connection between frames.

2. **High · AC-18 / AC-41: cached capability evidence can send a set to a one-repository receiver.** `apps/server/src/handoff/HandoffSetMoves.ts:357-370` trusts a previous answer for ten minutes, keyed only by environment. The new receiver also accepts set fields at `apps/server/src/handoff/HandoffReceiver.ts:403-410` without verifying that the source negotiated them. 3. **High · AC-18 / AC-41: retry can launch with a missing member or erase unrelated dirty work.** `apps/server/src/handoff/HandoffSetMoves.ts:563-570` identifies a prior attempt by directory name. Without a manifest, lines `634-646` treat a matching worktree as disposable; `apps/server/src/handoff/HandoffSetMoves.ts:593-599` force-removes it even if it has uncommitted edits.
