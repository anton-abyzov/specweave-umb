Where the policy comes from

Two options

A. Spec-owned, SpecWeave enforces Chosen

The policy travels in spec.md. SpecWeave's closure gate checks family, verdict and reviewed commit. The app routes reviews and shows the result.

## Review
- Reviewer: openai (gpt-6.1-sol)
- Depth: deep
- Required: yes
  • Holds from any tool: Codex, Claude Code, CLI, app.
  • Visible in the plan that agents and reviewers already read.
  • Records are files in the increment, reviewable in git.
  • Reviewer model is self-reported in --record; the card shows it.

B. App-owned review stage

The app stores verdicts in its database and its Close action waits for one. The policy lives only in Settings.

  • One click per review; strong UI.
  • Closing from the CLI, Codex or Claude Code outside the app skips it.
  • No per-increment ask; the spec cannot say "this one needs Sol".
  • State tied to one machine's database; handoffs lose it.
