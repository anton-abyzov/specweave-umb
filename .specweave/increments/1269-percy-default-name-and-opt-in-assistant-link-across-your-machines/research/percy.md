**Verdict: low risk.** That holds while "Percy" stays a default assistant label that users can rename inside SpecWithCode. It would rise to medium if "Percy" became a marketed feature or product name, such as "Meet Percy" headlines, a logo, a domain or an app-store listing.

**Evidence:**

- **BrowserStack Percy is the main conflict.** BrowserStack bought Percy (percy.io) in July 2020 and sells it as an AI-powered visual testing platform for CI pipelines. That is developer software, the same field as SpecWithCode. ([BrowserStack press release](https://www.browserstack.com/press/browserstack-acquires-percy-visual-testing), [Percy docs](https://browserstack.com/docs/percy/overview/visual-testing-basics))
- **BrowserStack's registration is likely but not confirmed.** One aggregator lists PERCY among BrowserStack Limited's registered US marks, and most of their 34 marks sit in Class 42. I could not get a serial number, class or status from a primary source, and the USPTO, Justia and that aggregator's pages were blocked or unreachable. ([trademarkdir aggregator](https://trademarkdir.com/owner/browserstack-limited)) The search found no EUIPO PERCY record covering software, but that does not rule one out.
- **Other US PERCY filings already exist in Classes 9/42:**
  - Serious Name Co., serial 99698112, voice garden-journal software. It had cleared publication review as of July 21, 2026. ([trademarkelite](https://www.trademarkelite.com/trademark/trademark-detail/99698112/PERCY))
  - DREAM TEAM BABY, serial 98398176. ([gleanmark](https://gleanmark.com/trademark/uspto-98398176))
  - BuyerMLS, serial 97370161. ([trademarkdir](https://trademarkdir.com/trademark/percy-97370161))

  With this many owners, the word alone is not strongly exclusive in software.
- **Other AI assistants already use the name.** Examples are Percy Chat (multi-model) ([percychat](https://percychat.featurebase.app/help/articles/9985974-welcome-to-percy-chat)), Footprint's Percy fraud-team assistant ([onefootprint](https://onefootprint.com/changelog/introducing-percy-our-new-ai-assistant-2)) and an older iOS ChatGPT client called "Percy - AI Assistant" ([reddit](https://reddit.ny4.dev/r/AppHookup/comments/12t75jx/iosapple_watch_percy_ai_assistant_percy_unlimited/jh6z9xm/?context=3)). Nobody owns "Percy" for assistants.
- **SEO collision is real but only matters for marketing.** Searches for "Percy" with dev or testing terms are dominated by BrowserStack ([GitHub percy](https://www.Github.com/percy), [webdriver.io](https://webdriver.io/docs/percy-overview)). SpecWithCode would not rank for "Percy". That is fine for an in-app label and a problem only if the name is promoted.

**Why low:** the name is a default persona label inside an MIT app. It is not a source identifier for the goods, and users can rename it. Trademark infringement depends on likely confusion about source, and a label the user renames, under SpecWithCode's own brand, gives little basis for it.

Keep it low by following four rules:

- Never write "Percy by SpecWithCode" or use "Percy" as a product or SKU name.
- Use no checkmark, magnifier or BrowserStack-like logo for the assistant.
- Avoid "visual testing" or "screenshot review" wording next to the name.
- In marketing, say "your assistant (default name Percy)".

This is a search-level check, not legal clearance. A paid USPTO/TSDR and EUIPO eSearch pull on "PERCY" in Classes 9 and 42 would confirm BrowserStack's registration details.
